Course project

Dormdry

A mobile app prototype designed to improve shared laundry services in student housing.

  • App Prototype
  • Student Housing
  • Laundry Management
Project details

Purpose: Reduce waiting, forgotten laundry, and confusion about shared washing machines in student housing.

Proposed features: Organize laundry queues, remind students to collect their clothes, and allow users to report faulty machines.

Status: A prototype demonstrating the proposed app experience, not a fully implemented laundry management service.
